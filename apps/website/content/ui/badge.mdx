### Rounded Variant

The `rounded` variant applies a full border-radius for a pill-like appearance.

<div className="flex flex-wrap gap-2 my-4">
  <Badge variant="rounded">Rounded</Badge>
  <Badge variant="rounded" className="bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-500 border-emerald-500/20">Rounded Success</Badge>
  <Badge variant="rounded" className="bg-amber-500/10 text-amber-500 border-amber-500/20">Rounded Warning</Badge>
</div>
